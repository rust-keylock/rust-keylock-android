<b>rust-keylock-android</b> is an app that provides access to the <a title="rust-keylock-lib" href="https://github.com/rust-keylock/rust-keylock-lib">rust-keylock</a> password manager in Android. 
<p/>
Its goals are to be:
<p/>
<ul>
<li>Secure</li>
<li>Simple to use</li>
<li>Portable</li>
<li>Extensible</li>
</ul>
<p>
<em>
Warning:
</em>
</p>
<p>
<em>
The project has not yet received any formal / official security reviews. Use it at your own risk.
</em>
</p>
